## 1. Who are the most prominent 紫微斗數 practitioners/teachers by audience, and how do they monetize?

### Takeaway
On YouTube, audience is concentrated in a few brands that grew out of TV. The largest are the TV show 命運好好玩 (69.1萬), ISZN 國際紫微學會, the 大耕老師 brand (61萬), and 簡少年 (39.5萬). After them comes a long tail of 1萬–9萬 紫微 channels. In Hong Kong the reach leaders are generalist 玄學 masters rather than 紫微 specialists: 陳定幫 (23.5萬), 李居明 (11.1萬) and 蘇民峰 (6.22萬). Leaders earn money in these ways:
- 1:1 readings, mostly NT$1,000–20,000 in Taiwan and HK$500–8,800 in Hong Kong
- multi-stage certification courses, from NT$12,000 per video stage to NT$44,800–150,000 cohort programmes
- an annual 流年 lecture (ISZN: NT$4,500)
- YouTube channel memberships
- books, products and events

## 2. Booking and client management: what do practitioners actually use (incl. LINE 官方帳號 pricing)?

### Takeaway
Booking in Taiwan is chat-first and manual. LINE 官方帳號 (or a personal LINE ID) is the front door. A typical flow is: price list in chat → form → bank transfer → confirm slot → reminder. Hong Kong uses WhatsApp; mainland and overseas clients use WeChat. Formal booking SaaS (Calendly, SimplyBook) was not visible among top 紫微 practitioners. Instead they use web forms, Google Forms and e-commerce checkouts (Shopify, EzyCourse). LINE OA's paid tiers went up on **2026-11-01**. 1:1 chat stays free.

## 3. Payments and invoicing in Taiwan (and Hong Kong/overseas)

### Takeaway
Taiwanese solo practitioners mostly take **bank transfers** (匯款) and reconcile them by hand. For card and online payment, the main official options are 綠界 ECPay (domestic card 2.75%, min NT$5, plus NT$1 per order) and 藍新 NewebPay (2.8% incl. tax, possibly outdated). **Stripe is not available to Taiwan businesses** but is in Hong Kong. PayPal serves overseas clients at roughly 4.4% plus a fixed fee (secondary sources). Recurring billing (定期定額) is supported by ECPay, but its fee is not published. Since 2025-01-01, individuals selling services online under NT$50,000 a month need not register for tax. Many solo practitioners therefore sit outside the e-invoice system.

- ECPay official fee table. All amounts exclude 5% business tax.

## 6. Content and marketing (platforms, AI content tools, seasonal peaks)

### Takeaway

## 8. Pain points practitioners publicly voice (and SaaS opportunities)

### Takeaway
The most visible pains are:
- AI substitution and price compression: 詹惟中 reports business down about 30% and has cut his entry price to NT$800; a HK teller claims 「零客」, zero walk-in clients
- dependence on IG, FB and YouTube for leads
- a crowded market with low trust: scams, marketplace complaints
- birth-time uncertainty, which forces a costly 定盤 workflow
- manual operations and limits on follow-up

No public complaints were found specifically about no-shows or report-writing time. Prepayment appears standard, which likely suppresses no-shows.

### Cited Findings
- AI impact in Taiwan: 詹惟中 (2026-03-05) says free AI fortune-telling is getting more accurate.
  - His business is down about 30%.
  - Businesses that charge for computer-generated readings face a foreseeable 「倒店潮」 (wave of closures).
  - He changed his pricing from a NT$5,800 12-palace reading to NT$800 per single-palace question with unlimited follow-ups. — [聯合報](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10089/9361681)
- Hong Kong (2026-10-21): an anonymous teller with about 20 years in the trade claims 「零客」 for the first time, blaming AI and the economy. Netizens mocked his ~HK$3,000 fee; some said AI readings were accurate and emotionally supportive **[unverified]**. — [unwire.hk](https://unwire.hk/2026/10/21/ai-fortune-teller-zero-customers/fun-tech/)
- Mainland: some diviners found DeepSeek outputs contained obvious errors. Another argued computer charting has not threatened the trade in two decades, because clients still pay for interpretation and service **[snippet]**. — [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30348122)
- Structural pains, from a 2025-04-06 industry analysis:
  - extremely high rivalry, with 「大者恆大」 (winner-takes-most)
  - AI-enabled 「幾週速成」 entrants using GPT and charting sites, some selling low-price AI readings
  - AI substitutes for information delivery
  - medium-high buyer power with low trust, so unbranded practitioners get price-cut
  - high platform dependence on IG, FB and YouTube, making an owned audience (email list, membership) a 「生死」 issue — [陳宏儒](https://psyhongju.com/ai-fortune-teller-career-paths/)
- Trust and scams: a LINE group charged at least NT$688 to join, and many payers never received a reading. — [Cofacts](https://en.cofacts.tw/article/7MC0J5oBWwAUNc5beMzz)
- 测测 has nearly 1,000 complaints about upselling, refunds and practitioner quality. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3699586209443457)

### Inferences
Mapping each pain to a 命理師工作台 feature (inference):

| Pain | Feature |
|---|---|
| AI price compression | AI first draft plus practitioner edit, which lowers the cost per report and makes NT$800–999 written products profitable |
| Platform dependence | Owned client list and branded report pages that bring clients back to LINE |
| Birth-time uncertainty | Rectification workflow: life-event form, candidate charts, true solar time and birthplace correction |
| Follow-up limits | Paid follow-up Q&A on a client portal |
| Manual operations | LINE-native intake, ECPay pay-links and ATM reconciliation, automatic reminders |
| Trust | Professional white-label PDFs; transparent policies such as guarantees and refunds |

On consumer subscription versus workspace, the evidence cuts both ways:
- **Against consumer-first:**
  - Free and cheap AI readings are already pulling consumers away from paid readings (詹惟中, the HK teller).
  - Marketplaces at scale (测测) attract complaints.
  - Consumers distrust "computer readings", and 詹惟中 explicitly predicts closures for paid computer-reading businesses.
- **For the workspace:**
  - Practitioners are under pressure to raise throughput and differentiate.
  - They already pay for LINE OA, course platforms and chart apps.
  - A large and growing supply of newly certified practitioners lacks tooling (194 registered businesses plus many unregistered solo practitioners).

This weighing is inference.

### Gaps
- No public data on no-show rates or no-show complaints. Prepayment is the observed norm (天機, 林子秦, 無相閣).
- No practitioner statements were found quantifying time spent writing reports.
- No surveys of practitioners' software spend in Taiwan or Hong Kong.
- The 財政部 figure (194 businesses, NT$2.9億) likely excludes unregistered solo practitioners. The total number of practising 紫微 practitioners in Taiwan is unknown.
